How the step view behaves

  • It covers the chat, not the page. The canvas stays visible with the step highlighted. × or "Back to the conversation" returns you exactly where you were in the chat.
  • Values are grouped by how they were decided: needs you, measured, settled by a rule (folded). Clicking any value shows its reason and citation in place, as the pipeline file does.
  • A question has one answer, wherever you give it. Answer strandedness here and the chat gets "Got it: reverse-stranded, set on SUBREAD_FEATURECOUNTS". The dock clears too.
  • The text box stays, and it's about this step. "Ask about this step…" sends your message to the chat with the step attached, then closes the sheet so you see the answer.
  • The tools from today's Step tab are kept (why this tool, swap, remove). Removing a step goes through the chat's confirmation ("So you never want counting here, right?"), as plan 2 built.

On a phone

  • The chat is the main view; Canvas and Understood are tabs beside it. The open-question dock and the queue look the same as on desktop.
