The parts

  1. Canvas is the same living canvas from plan 2. The step on offer is dashed. A step the chat just changed glows briefly (TRIMGALORE here).
  2. Step strip takes over from the Step tab: a slim strip pinned above the chat. Each step is clickable and opens that step over the chat, with a × to go back.
  3. Announcement: every change made outside the chat gets one line, written by the engine, linking to where it happened.
  4. The offer is a message, not a separate panel.
  5. The question is a message with answers built in, laid out like the Question board: each answer has its one-line note; What is this? and which steps read it sit one click away. The four provenance tags fold into one quiet line.
  6. Your queued message, typed while the model was still working. It shows at once, marked waiting, and is answered in order (your choice: queue).
  7. The composer never locks. Today it reads Waiting for the answer… and refuses input.

One choice on this screen: how heavy should a question look?

The mockup above uses A. Click one, or tell me in the terminal.

A

A card in the stream

A bordered box like the Question board: question in bold, the reason under it, then answer rows with notes. Questions stand out clearly from conversation, but a long gathering phase is a column of boxes.

B

A plain message with answer chips

The question reads like any other assistant message, with answers as chips underneath and notes revealed on hover or tap. It feels more like a chat, but a question is easier to miss in a long thread.

C

Message in the stream, answers pinned above the composer

The question is a normal message where it was asked, and the open question's answers dock above the input box until answered. You can't lose it after chatting, and the "Still need…" reminder isn't needed. The cost: two places show the same question.
