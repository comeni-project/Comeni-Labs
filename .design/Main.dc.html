Comeni Labs · theme, round two
One run,
five atmospheres
Same screen on every board, so the only variable is the world it lives in. Round one was five palette sheets and it had no pulse; these are five atmospheres over a real run.
The five
CONSOLE
Alive by streaming. True black, scanlines, a light pass, a blinking tail. Your pick from round one, kept and given air.
PHOSPHOR
Alive by glow. One amber tube. Brightness is the encoding, so duration reads before you get to a number.
DEEP FIELD
Alive by slow motion. Enormous arcs breathing at 5% behind hairline marks. Quiet, and the only one that reads expensive.
FOUNDRY
Alive by heat. Grain, an ember pooling under the page. Finished work cools to steel; only what runs is lit.
VELLUM
Alive by texture. Halftone, paper tooth, a second plate slightly out of register. The one light option, so the choice is real.
Four marks, four questions
Nothing is drawn that does not answer something. RED and the four golden signals, read for a batch run:
WATERFALL
Where is the time going? — once, at the top.
BULLET
Am I over-asking? — per process, held against touched.
TRACE
Is it still moving? — only on a running row.
COUNT
Did anything fail? — a number and a word, not a chart.
No sparkline on a finished row, no donut of tiers, no tile that restates the number beside it.
The one finding worth keeping
Three of four processes reserve about four times the memory they touch. That is the most useful sentence on the screen, and it exists because the resource mark was chosen to answer a question rather than to fill a panel.
