## The findings, by shape

Two clean shapes, and they are worth keeping distinct because the fixes differ.

**A guard checks a string's *shape*, not its *content* (A44–A49).** Plan 1.10 made `pipeline.yml`
hand-editable and let registry-authored strings flow verbatim into generated Groovy.
- **A44 (critical)** — `test_data` reaches `nextflow.config` unescaped and executes at
  `nextflow config` time. Reachable by hand-edit *and* by an untrusted `--registry` overlay, and
  it crosses the publication door.
- **A45** — `NfTemplate` validates only newlines; the text around `{value}` injects shell and Groovy.
- **A46 (critical)** — a tier-4 answer has two homes; `emit` reads `settings[].value`, `upgrade`
  reads `decisions[].human_override`, and they can disagree — one file, two pipelines.
- A47 emit erases a gate verdict; A48 a missing `goal:` upgrades to empty; A49 a refused emit
  half-writes then blames the user.

**A fact is computed and never carried to where it is read (A50–A54).**
- **A50 (critical)** — `mendel publish` is `upgrade` with every brake removed: it re-resolves
  against the installed registry, erases human overrides, stamps a gate and writes in place, all
  behind a report gated to the other verb. The door with no undo, certifying a pipeline nobody read.
- A51 four-kind displacements dropped to two; A52 a duplicate decision key discards an override;
  A53 `upgrade --out` destroys any *other* pipeline's directory; A54 a resolver may claim
  `source: HUMAN` and empty `needs_review()`.

**A38 (critical)** stands on its own: two of the three declared emission routes (`via: meta`,
`via: directive`) are validated, recorded with provenance, and emit nothing — issue #10 reopened
one level down, and #10 is recorded as closed.
