# Settings, to the board — plan

**Goal:** Settings as the board draws it (artboard "4 · Settings", copy in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-frontend-redesign-board/Settings.dc.html`): a page, its sections
in the rail, every section on one scrolling page.
**Approved:** the operator, 2026-11-17 ("settings needs to be done").

## Decisions

- The page stays drawn from what the servers declare (`GET /settings`, Wiener's settings) through
  `SettingRow`; nothing is named that the server does not serve. Board sections group the served
  ones: General (`system.author`, appearance), Building, Models, Running (Wiener's), Data & privacy
  (privacy, retention), Registry, About (system, developer). A section the board does not place
  keeps its own title, after these.
- `/settings` replaces the `?settings=` overlay; `#<section>` links a section. The overlay,
  `SettingsLink` and the shell's overlay handling are deleted (no users before v0.1.0).
- Board items with no served setting are left out: the privacy levels list, Running's
  target/folders rows, telemetry.
