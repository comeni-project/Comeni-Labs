## The renumbering, and why it is not cosmetic

The plan at `2026-08-04-publication-and-the-registry-split.md` was called **Plan 2.5** and
ran at position 5, before Plan 2 at position 6. Asked why, the honest answer was: **nothing
said.** `notes/README.md` justified Plan 1.5 before it, and `conformance.md` §7
justified Plan 1.6 before the forge — but no document anywhere gave a reason for publication
preceding the forge. The index asserted an order and it was believed.

The name was the cause. `2.5` recorded when the plan was *written* — after Plan 2 existed —
and everyone read it as when it should *run*. This repository already knows this failure
mode and warns about it one line above the offending table: *"Filenames are a log, not an
order."* The plan numbers had exactly the same problem and nobody had noticed, because a
number looks like a sequence in a way a date does not.

**`1.7` is the accurate name.** Plans 1.5, 1.6 and 1.7 are the deterministic core — no AI, no
network, no new dependency. Plan 2 is AI and the forge. The number now encodes the order
instead of contradicting it.

Renamed across 21 files. **The three journal entries were not touched** — they are
append-only and were correct on their dates, which is what this entry is for.

## What the ordering rationale actually is, now written down

`notes/README.md` gained a *Why that order* section. Reconstructed, not invented:

**For 1.7 before 2.** Plan 2 was written 2026-08-02, before most of the types it references
existed — the index already said so and nobody had drawn the conclusion. Plan 1.7 was written
2026-08-04 against real code. Every plan here has needed correcting mid-execution (six steps
in the measurements plan, five in 1.5, six in 1.6), so the fresher plan is the cheaper one.
And 1.7 is pure-package work, where Plan 2 stands up `mendel-ai`, LiteLLM and model access,
opening three of the four egress doors at once.

**Against, and it is recorded rather than buried.** Nothing in Plan 1.7 moves the v1 success
criterion — its one unmet clause is the plain-language prompt, which is Plan 2 Task 3. And
`mendel publish` is the door with no undo, built for pipelines drawn from a registry the
forge has not filled. **If v1 becomes the priority, this order is the thing to revisit.**

## Found while writing it down

**Plan 1.7 Task 5 and Plan 2 Task 4 both build replay.** `replay.py` in `mendel-resolver`
versus `ReplayingResolver` in `mendel-ai`. They are not the same thing — one replays recorded
decisions when a curated bundle is edited, the other caches model answers across runs — but
they are close enough that building both without noticing yields two ways to do one job.
Recorded in the index: whichever runs second absorbs the first. Not filed as an issue; it
lives where the person executing either plan will read it.

This is the second time in two days that writing down a justification found something. The
first was `conformance.md` §9, where naming the ratchet's *limit* — that a test profile
cannot glob over https, and no static check will ever catch it — was more useful than the
list of what it does catch.

## Traps

  about their dates. `notes/README.md` is the current name and order.
- **Plan numbers are not a schedule either.** The lesson generalises past this one rename: if

  is real. For filenames this repo already knew. For plan numbers it did not.

  that order*, and it is not rhetorical — v1 runs through Plan 2, not Plan 1.7.
