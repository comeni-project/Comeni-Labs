## The three things a fresh reader will get wrong

**`make check` is not verification.** `make verify`. Every part gated on it and the counts
matrix caught nothing — which is the point: A23's mechanism is exactly what
`tests/test_counts.py` asserts, so a green run there is evidence rather than ceremony.

**Every loader takes layer roots now.** `MeasurementRegistry.load("registry")`, not
`load("registry/measurements")`. A loader handed a slice of a layer cannot know which layer
it is reading, which is the whole of A23 and A24. 40-odd call sites moved; all were tests.

**`resolve()` takes a required keyword-only `vocabulary`.** 43 call sites. An optional
guard is the guard the next verb forgets, and `mendel upgrade` — whose goal comes from a
stranger's bundle — is the verb that keeps being the one that forgets.

## What reverting found that reading did not

Five guards were inert or unjustified, and **three of the five were in code written the same
week**:

1. `stack()`'s `origin[key] != layer.index` — a condition that can never be false. Now an
   assertion, like the `min`/`max` before it.
2. `_is_identifier`'s `isascii()` — a narrowing with neither a test nor a justification.
   Groovy *does* allow unicode identifiers; the refusal is deliberate and now says so.
3. **A21**, twice. The forgery test restated the entry format by hand, so reverting the fix
   left 12 passed. The first rewrite called `entry_hash` and *still* passed, because it
   computed the content half without `_FILE`. Both halves have to come from the code.
4. **A36** — reverting `_FILE` itself fails nothing. Recorded, not fixed: it guards against
   a second entry kind and `_LINK` went away with A9.
5. **A37** — `producers_of` dropping priority entirely fails nothing, including the test
   named for the property. The fixture's higher-priority contract also sorted first by id.

And one test of mine asserted over an empty loop (A33's first version, no tie in the goal),
which is the 2026-08-06 audit's `_source_for` defect committed again by someone who had read
about it that morning.

> **The lesson, stated once more.** Reading a guard tells you what it *claims*. Only
> breaking the code tells you what it *does*. Care catches neither an inert guard nor a
> fixture that agrees with itself.

## Decisions made while executing

**Do not reconstruct the past — record it** (A28). The bundle carries `Emitted`: the digests
of the files it was published from. Re-emitting the old IR needs the registry as it was, and
a contract that has left the registry is one of the two cases `upgrade` exists to report, so
the obvious implementation dies on exactly it. `drift_against` had that bug in Plan 1.7.

**A guard that reports its own blind spot.** When the digest moves and `diff_ir` has nothing
to say, `upgrade` says so and names both causes — a compiler change, or a gap in the diff.
A14's class, closed structurally for one case rather than by protocol.

**Shape does not separate an edge reference from a filename.** Root E's spec predicted
`SourceDecision(chosen="run.cram")` would be refused; it is not, and cannot be —
`<identifier>.<identifier>` is exactly what `dual.bam` is. Written into `marks.py` where the
next reader meets it. What the shape does refuse is every filename with a second dot or a
hyphen; the candidate check does the rest.

**The anchor hypothesis is dismissed, measured.** Merge keys — the only anchor form with
A31's shape — are already refused by `SafeLoader`. Aliases share rather than copy, so the
expansion bomb does not amplify, and a real loader refuses it in 10ms because
`frozenset[str]` is not a nested list.

**Six free-text fields, not two.** `CLAUDE.md` invariant 14 said two for a plan and a half.
The guard holds the list literally and is the honest count; the prose is what drifts.

## Traps

- **Push the branch.** Everything is local.
- **`ruff --fix` will merge an `if`/`elif` into one `or` expression** and hand you a
  100-character line that changes nothing semantically and reads like nothing. It did that
  to `test_construction.py`; the readable version is committed.
- **`git checkout <file>` reverts your uncommitted work, not just your probe.** It ate Part
  E's `resolve.py` edits mid-revert. Delete the probe with a script instead.
- **`Displacement.displaced_keys` is `list[Subject]`, not `list[ContractId]`.** The moment
  `ContractId` got a shape validator, the synthetic keys `test_layered.py` stacks stopped
  validating — and those exist precisely so the mechanism can be tested without a registry.
- **A `Literal` discriminator is permitted by the egress leaf rule**, because it is narrower
  than the enum it draws from. That is new and worth not re-litigating.
