## Decisions, and why

- **Per installation now, per person with sign-in** (operator). There are no accounts to key a
  person's value on.
- **The environment is a lock, not a seed** (operator). A seed makes `.env` silently stop
  working after the first save; a lock keeps deploy files the truth and lets a policy be held.
- **Connections and a model per purpose now, not later** (operator): changing the shape of
  stored settings later costs more than building it now.
- **Pacing and tier 4 are declared but greyed** until 14.7.8 reads them: a control nothing reads
  would be false.
- **Telemetry is reported under Running**, because Wiener holds it (approved with the plans).
- **The want stays on gemma3:12b** until #198 measures accuracy (operator, option C). qwen2.5:7b
  is the candidate: it fits the card and is ~4× faster, but only shape was measured.

## Traps

- **nginx's config is baked into the `web` image.** A new route needs `docker compose build
  web`, not a restart.
- **`docker compose up -d` after a compose change**, or `COMENI_SETTINGS_KEY` and the
  `COMENI_AI_MODEL_<NAME>` pins never reach the containers.
- **A `grep` at the end of an `&&` chain hides a failing test** and lets the commit through.
  Run the suite on its own, then commit.
- **The session scratchpad does not survive a restart.** Post measurements to the issue first.
