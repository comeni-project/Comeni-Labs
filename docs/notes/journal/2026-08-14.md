## What the audit found, in one paragraph

The product claim split cleanly in two, and stating the halves separately is the most useful thing
it produced. **"Same goal in → same pipeline out" holds**, and is stronger than
`ARCHITECTURE.md` §8 claims — byte-identical across two `PYTHONHASHSEED` values and across a
re-spelled goal, attacked independently by two streams and unmoved. **"Nothing was guessed
silently" does not currently hold**: of the six values that reach the generated Nextflow in a
default build, five carry no `why:` at all, including the strandedness flag this repository uses
as its worked example of the system working. Seven roots, all repairable with fields and checks on
types that already exist.

**Read A92 first regardless of the rest.** `emit._argument()` joins two ports with `.combine()`,
a cross product: two samples in, four processes out, half of them pairing one sample's data with
another's, exit 0, no warning. It is the only finding that produces *wrong results from a green
run*, and `--gate test` is structurally blind to it because the nf-core test dataset has one
sample. The repair needs a two-sample fixture attached to it or the class stays invisible.

## Verification discipline, because it is the point of a synthesiser

The brief's rule was applied literally: **a finding the synthesiser has not reproduced is
PLAUSIBLE, not a result.** 25 of 44 are CONFIRMED first-hand; 19 are carried as PLAUSIBLE with the
reviewer's demonstration behind them. Nine of the ten criticals are in the confirmed set —
**A119 is the exception and should be reproduced before Root 5's repair is scoped.**

Reproduction changed three findings, which is the argument for doing it:

- **A94 downgraded** — the `params.<name>` collision is real but fires only when the vocabulary
  declares no `entry_channel`, which the finding did not mention.
- **A90 softened** — `diff_pipeline` does miss `call`, but the blind-spot verdict is a *designed*
  backstop for exactly that gap and is what noticed. "Silently reverts" overstates it.
- **A79 re-framed, and this is the interesting one.** The shipped registry does cite the STAR
  paper as the reason HISAT2 was chosen — but the rule author's own comment shows the block `cite`
  was written to justify the *decision axis*, for which Dobin et al. is fair. The defect is that
  `because()` collapses axis-justification into row-justification. Harder problem, better finding.

## The decision: Mendel is the engine, the AI is its primary operator

Made today, on reading the audit's philosophy section, and recorded in
`docs/design/mendel.md` §1 with the reasoning, `notes/README.md` with the argument against,
and `docs/reference/pipeline-schema.md` where it changes how the artifact is read.

The synthesis had compared the artifact to a chat window and asked whether a bench scientist could
follow it. That compares a substrate to a product — through Plan 1 there is no AI layer at all.
Everything built so far is the **engine**; a human can drive it, but the intended operator is the
AI, and `pipeline.yml` is the **save file** it sets down, picks up, tunes and re-emits rather than
carrying a pipeline in its context.

**It costs no invariant.** An agent driving the CLI is a user of it, outside the engine, exactly as
a human at a terminal is. Invariant 3 constrains what Mendel calls, not who calls Mendel. The
three runtime AI points stay three; the four doors stay four. A model placed *inside* the engine to
tune an artifact would be a fourth point and a new door, and must be argued separately rather than
inherited from this.

**What it changes is severity, and it cuts the same direction the audit already found.** Three
findings are promoted from legibility to correctness, because the failure mode becomes a machine's
rather than a person's: **A104/A105/A77** (a human leaves a stale `why:` occasionally and may
notice; an agent does it systematically and does not), **A106** (a person reading a blank asks, a
model reading a blank *fills it* — confabulation with a deterministic pipeline lending it
credibility), and **A130** (nothing distinguishes a model-authored `why.reason` from a
human-authored one, which is load-bearing under `guarded` and `sealed`).

scoping it, because it is ordered by what gets more expensive once the forge drafts contracts at
volume rather than by severity. Two of the repairs are Plan-2 blockers outright: **A129** (two of

needed contortion, and **nine cannot be written at all** — and the nine are the specification for
